# UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTION & FRAUD POLICY DOCUMENT

Section 1: Mandatory Escalation & Freeze Protocol
- ANY complaint reporting unrecognized charges, suspected fraudulent activity, stolen credentials, or unauthorized transactions MUST BE ESCALATED IMMEDIATELY TO HUMAN REVIEW.
- AI automated systems MUST NOT automatically issue refunds for unauthorized transaction claims without human agent verification and security audit.

Section 2: Customer Protection & Dispute Freeze
- Upon filing an unauthorized transaction report:
  1. The affected transaction is placed on security freeze.
  2. The account protection team investigates device fingerprinting, IP logs, and OTP records.
  3. Resolution timeline for fraud investigation: 3 to 7 business days.
