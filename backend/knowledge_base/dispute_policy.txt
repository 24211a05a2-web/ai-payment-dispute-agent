# GENERAL DISPUTE RESOLUTION POLICY DOCUMENT

Section 1: AI Dispute Handling Guidelines
- Disputes are processed through automated policy retrieval and database cross-verification.
- High confidence decisions (>= 80%) with clear database evidence (e.g. Payment SUCCESS + Order FAILED) are resolved automatically.
- Uncertain, ambiguous, missing transaction, or high-value cases are routed to Human Review (Status: ESCALATED / UNDER_REVIEW).

Section 2: Ticket SLAs and Tracking
- Every dispute filed receives a unique Ticket ID (e.g., DSP-10025).
- Automated resolutions update status instantly.
- Human review cases are attended within 24 hours.
