# REFUND POLICY DOCUMENT

Section 1: Order Failures & Payment Deductions
- If a customer's payment status is SUCCESS but the associated order status is FAILED or CANCELLED, the customer is eligible for a full 100% refund.
- Automatic refunds are processed within 24 to 48 hours to the original payment method.
- If the payment was deducted and the order failed, refund eligibility is AUTOMATICALLY APPROVED.

Section 2: Processing Timelines
- Standard banking refund timeline: 3-5 business days for Credit/Debit Cards, 24-48 hours for UPI and Netbanking.
- Instant wallet refunds are credited immediately.

Section 3: Partial & Conditional Refunds
- Partial refunds apply if items are missing from an order.
- Non-refundable convenience or service fees may apply unless the failure was caused by system error.
