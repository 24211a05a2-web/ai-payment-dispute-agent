# PAYMENT FAILURE POLICY DOCUMENT

Section 1: Technical Payment Failures
- When a transaction status shows FAILED in the system, no money should be debited from the customer's account.
- If money was debited during a transaction marked FAILED by the payment gateway, it is classified as an "In-Flight Auto-Reversal Delay".
- Banking networks automatically reverse such debits within 24 to 72 hours without requiring manual intervention.

Section 2: System Timeouts & Retries
- System timeouts during checkout may result in temporary authorization holds.
- These holds are released automatically by the issuing bank within 3 business days if no charge confirmation is sent.
