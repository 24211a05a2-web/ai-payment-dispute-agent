# DUPLICATE PAYMENT POLICY DOCUMENT

Section 1: Duplicate Charges Detection
- When multiple identical transaction amounts are charged to a customer within a 60-minute window for a single order ID, the system flags the secondary charge as a duplicate.
- Duplicate charges are eligible for immediate automated reversal to the original payment source.

Section 2: Verification Protocol
- Automated dispute AI cross-references reference IDs and timestamp gaps.
- Confirmed duplicate payments are refunded within 24 hours without penalty.
